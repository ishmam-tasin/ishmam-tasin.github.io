# Tasin Research Hub

Create a clean, professional, academic personal website for **Md Ishmam Tasin**, a Computer Science researcher from Bangladesh working at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Usable Security & Privacy, and socio-technical systems**.

The website is primarily an **academic research portfolio**, intended for:

* PhD admissions committees
* Professors and potential research collaborators
* Researchers in HCI, usable security/privacy, ICTD/HCI4D, and related areas
* Students interested in my research

The website should communicate that I am an emerging researcher developing a research identity around **how people understand, interact with, and respond to security- and privacy-sensitive technologies**, particularly when those technologies are complex, unfamiliar, emerging, or used in underrepresented contexts.

Use the information from my attached CV as the primary source for all factual content. **Do not invent publications, research contributions, awards, positions, research findings, faculty affiliations, or other credentials.**

# 20. Visual Design

Use a restrained academic visual system.

### Typography

Use a highly readable academic font.

Possible choices:

* Inter
* Source Sans 3
* IBM Plex Sans
* or a similarly clean sans-serif

Alternatively, use a subtle serif for headings if it improves the academic feel.

Avoid overly decorative fonts.

### Colors

Use a restrained palette:

* White/off-white background
* Dark charcoal text
* One subtle accent color
* Light gray borders/dividers

Avoid:

* neon colors
* gradients
* excessive blue/purple AI-style gradients
* glassmorphism
* excessive shadows
* colorful cards

### Layout

Use generous whitespace.

Content width should be approximately 1100–1200px.

Make paragraphs comfortable to read.

Use clear section headings.

Use thin dividers where useful.
